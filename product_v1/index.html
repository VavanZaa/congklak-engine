Choose a nonempty pit on your side. Sow around both rows, skipping your opponent's store. An occupied final pit relays its seeds; a final seed in your own store gives another turn. An empty pit on your side captures its nonempty opposite. An empty row passes, and the round ends when both rows are empty.

This table follows the declared Betawi seven-pit, single-round profile. Simultaneous openings, refills and closed pits are outside this version. Analyses save each completed depth. After a restart, open a saved analysis and choose Resume.
