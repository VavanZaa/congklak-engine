# Revised Q-stability producer

This prospective study measures one fixed heuristic on 16 separately reserved assessment roots. It performs bounded H_v1 reference searches only. Model files, policy/trunk, optimizer weights, production sampling and allocation HOLD stay fixed. No neural inference, training, games, self-play, pruning or promotion is part of this producer. The authoritative protocol is `docs/QSTABILITY_SCOPE.md`.

The metadata inventory derives from the accepted `stratum-inventory.json` component graph and its reservation overlay. It checks their actual baseline file hashes on every admission. Removing all consumed stratum components leaves 3,772 raw states, 531 components, 535 representative families and 536 families across every retained origin, with five repeated raw-state origin observations. The inherited graph already excludes allocation reservations, true evaluation, forensic, mixed-role and historical H12/H14-exposed components. The independent audit rederives that entire protection chain. No target body or SQLite journal is read by this selection path.

One salted SHA matching selects two roots in each of four strata by two actors, with 16 globally distinct origin components/families. Full selected component support is reserved for future optimizers before measurement. All original source occurrences and historical training-candidate roles remain visible. Previous H8/H10 exposure is allowed and disclosed; neither absence of ancestral training nor prefix-disjoint independence is claimed.

For every root, the raw reliability is `max(1/4,1/(1+max_legal_abs(Q10-Q8)/4))`. Each cell receives mass 1/8, distributed in proportion to its two raw reliabilities; the comparator is uniform 1/16. Exact rational arithmetic and all-action support are mandatory. The full endpoint-linked realized rule freezes before H12, with immutable `references-prefix.sqlite3` and `references-manifest.json`. These phase files and the realized rule are required evidence after freezing; they are not disposable exports. H12 cannot define weights. A maximum driven by a weak alternative remains part of the fixed rule, with action-level diagnostics rather than post-outcome adjustment.

All 16 H8 attempts precede all 16 H10 attempts. Each has a 10-million-node cap, time cutoff zero, TT16MiB, PVS and 512MiB memory. If all 32 endpoints are complete, `realize()` publishes exact weights and the immutable prefix. Independent reference method04 must explicitly attest `H12_ready=True`, the 32 endpoint hashes and all three immutable phase files before any of the 16 H12 attempts. Each H12 has the same cap; total requested production work is at most 480 million nodes. No retry with a larger cap, replacement, fallback depth or budget expansion is allowed. Every full legal-action requested label and consecutive iteration history is linked to the exact raw source candidate, separately from the new wrapper hash.

If H8/H10 is incomplete after all 32 attempts, `run()` closes status `references_incomplete_hold`, `protocol_closed=True`, `assessment_available=False`, `H12_held=True`. No realized rule, weighted probability, fabricated weighted mass or H12 attempt exists. `assess()` retains known uniform 1/16 and null weighted mass/full risk with an unavailable HOLD screen. The pure `qstability_metrics` helper defines the exact predeclared Q-drift, max-Q, root-drift, entire-best-set regret, capture, ordinary-cell, fixed-mass, nontriviality and inverse-concentration ESS guards. ESS describes probability concentration, not independent examples or uncertainty. A favorable result authorizes neither applying weights nor training.

Stages remain explicit: `all` cannot bypass realization and independent H12 admission.
