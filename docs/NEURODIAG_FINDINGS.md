# Existing-source neural diagnostic findings

The fixed models have almost the same target fit on this panel. No observation meets the declared tanh-saturation threshold, and the shared representation retains substantial variation. The results identify target-horizon changes, dependent self-play outcome variation and limited in-sample logit representability; they do not establish why earlier training or timed play succeeded or failed, or justify another training run.

These findings use the independently certified cached [result](../build/neurodiag-result.json), [production checks](../build/neurodiag-audit/production.json) and [observation witness](../build/neurodiag-audit/observation-witness.json). Interpretation used saved JSON only: no additional encoder, model, gradient, SVD, search, label or game execution. Both models, their shared six trunk/policy tensors and all earlier ownership protections remain fixed. The support consists of eligible **training-source** rows, not a new assessment partition or unseen validation set. The [scope](NEURODIAG_SCOPE.md) and [metric definitions](NEURODIAG_METRICS.md) specify selection, arithmetic and limits.

## Target fit

Production committed 20 batches: 308 distinct raw-state/feature units, 308 shared trunk and policy evaluations, 616 value-head/model-view evaluations and 360 individual target records from 332 source records. Engineering fixtures and independent recomputations are separate work. Thirteen missing paired feature vectors were generated by the pure literal encoder; all 15 existing paired caches were checked. No Native or Torch execution, optimizer step, new teacher label, PUCT search or game was performed.

## Target changes are separate from prediction errors

H8 and H10 differ at 23 of the 28 paired roots. Absolute target shift has mean 3 seeds, median 2 and maximum 9. For example, `midgame-history-pilot/20261023/84` changes from H8=42 to H10=33. The same fixed predictions consequently have different errors against the two horizons. These are different finite-horizon references; the deeper value is not established truth, and the 26-high/2-low panel does not estimate a balanced ordinary population.

Of the 24 paired self-play starts, 22 have different observed returns. Within-start spread has mean 13.6667 seeds, median 9 and maximum 56. The largest pair, `ordinary-selfplay-history/20261026/264`, returns 70 and 14; another returns 16 and 60. Both outcomes are retained individually with the same initial features. They are two dependent fixed-control-policy trajectories, not two independent labels of a minimax value. Their empirical conflict cannot be removed by calling either return the unique truth; it also does not estimate a calibrated noise floor or prove that additional rollouts would improve strength.

## Saturation, sensitivity and shared features

All 360 normalized targets are strict interior targets; none is an endpoint or meets the near-boundary threshold `abs(z)>=.95`. Neither fixed head meets `abs(tanh(u))>=.95` at any of the 308 units. Mean normalized output gain `1-tanh(u)^2` is control/candidate 0.917749/0.917873 on base H8, 0.969133/0.969707 on paired roots and 0.959542/0.959437 on self-play starts. Saturated tanh outputs therefore are not observed as a broad explanation on this support. This does not establish successful optimization or healthy gradients on every possible state.

There are no recorded near-kink preactivations at the fixed `1e-8` threshold. Every first-layer unit activates somewhere. Four second-layer neurons, zero-based indices 26, 29, 32 and 46, never activate on these 308 inputs. On the primary 256 roots, centered encoded-feature rank is 112/127 and centered hidden rank is 60/64. The 14 hypothetical-choice terminal flags are all zero on this support, and the count/active inputs contain algebraic dependence; rank deficiency is not by itself an encoder failure. The identified-subspace condition numbers are 245.61 for encoded features and 47.33 for hidden features, not finite condition numbers for the deficient full column spaces.

Fixed-head normalized-Huber gradient cancellation ratios on base H8 are 0.0390 for control and 0.0523 for candidate. Individual row gradients can point in opposing directions even when the averaged gradient is small. These cached derivatives are local continuous encoded-feature sensitivities at fixed parameters, not historical training gradients, causal board-feature importance or a proposed update. Seed sensitivities additionally scale with signed active seeds; they cannot be compared across bands as though that scale were absent.

## What the projection does and does not show

For the global primary 256-root group, `[hidden64,1]` has rank 61 of 65 at the frozen `rcond=1e-10`. Its weighted **logit-space** residual RMSE against `atanh((target-d)/r)` is 0.163494, with logit-target RMS 0.339904 and retained-subspace condition number 213.14. Thus these exact interior target logits do not all lie in this frozen affine-hidden span at the declared tolerance. This is in-sample preactivation representability, not a seed-error capacity lower bound, evidence that a different trunk would help, or a fitted new head. No affine head coefficients were constructed or persisted.

Each 28-root paired-horizon projection has full row rank 28 and residual about `3e-16`. That is the explicitly flagged small-sample interpolation case, not proof of useful learning capacity. The self-play design has rank 24 for 48 scalar targets because each start repeats the same hidden features; its residual RMSE is 0.215190 in logit units. Distinct observed returns at an identical feature vector cannot both be matched by one deterministic output. That empirical observation remains specific to these dependent outcomes and this logit-space calculation; it is not a generalization or seed-error bound.

## Bounded next work and retained evidence

Keep the current models, policy, sampler and engine settings. A future separately authorized neural experiment should first preregister the target estimand and family-level validation, retain horizon/return-dispersion controls, and specify a small comparison that can distinguish representation changes from target/optimization effects. It would still need equal-time evidence against both baselines and exact-endgame nonregression before any promotion decision. The present analysis does not choose a new architecture, loss weight, optimizer, extra data budget or another head-only training run; the existing [neural work plan](REGRET_NEURAL_NEXT.md) remains prospective.

Both four-method owner suites passed their first frozen runs. The initial independent production execution failed an audit-only literal-role expectation (`training` versus the saved `train`); its exact source, report and logs are retained under [the failure record](../build/neurodiag-audit-production-role-failure-v1/diagnostic.json). A separately frozen audit version then passed both production methods and certified the unchanged cache.
