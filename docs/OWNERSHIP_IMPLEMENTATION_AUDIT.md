## Findings and repairs

Historical training exposure was initially classified as an optimizer exclusion
for the expressly reused support pool. A versioned role adapter corrects only
those tags; genuine evaluation and forensic conflicts remain on HOLD.

The original launcher checked the default completed output before routing
watchdog management. That could strand a new worker before its permit. The V2
launcher routes management first and binds the actual worker, watchdog, output,
source files and lease. The stranded original lease was conservatively charged;
its accounting was not reset. Independent V2 recovery cases exercised the
repaired route while the original default completed output already existed.

The first prospective metadata publisher computed chunk hashes using LF bytes
but published CRLF bytes on Windows. The first recovery fixture therefore
correctly refused its own committed chunk. That attempt constructed 128 counters
and made 571 rule probes. Its source, approval, chunks, journal and failure
receipt remain intact. The V2 publisher writes exact UTF-8/LF bytes, fsyncs and
renames them. A new source-bound pre-draw approval preceded every V2 draw; the
constructor, population, salts, matcher and quotas were unchanged.

The first observer accepted a forged passed-only closure with an empty evidence
map. The V2 observer requires the acceptance schema, independent audit groups,
actual recovery counts, current required evidence, frozen model hashes and
final-progress bindings. The original shortcut was reproduced in a mock fixture
and the repaired observer returned HOLD for the same forgery.

Five auditor fixture corrections are retained separately: a pool-key typo,
optional-unit-field handling, missing isolated marker vertices in the independent
graph, comparison of volatile physical report fields, and an incorrect expected
choice count at the handoff. Their original sources and logs were preserved.
They do not replace or conceal the real publisher or observer defects.

The first final seal also refused a stale observer-test pin after the root
finalizer changed. The earlier receipt was retained and only the dependent
observer checks were rerun against the frozen final sources before sealing.

## Remaining admission limits

Some earlier native smoke and failed timing attempts did not persist their full
traces. The worker kill immediately before decision publication also loses that
particular uncommitted envelope; its expected fixed-work footprint has saved
duplicates, but its actual capture is not claimed. The missing-attempt ledgers
and global training/evaluation HOLD remain. The supplement protects actual
recorded origin/state incidences and standalone state markers without inventing
unknown ancestry. Reservations certify separation from the known recorded graph
only, not historical absence of every overlap.

The 320 genuine support conflicts still prevent training admission. Balanced
custom starts are the prospectively declared population; reachability from the
standard opening and broad natural-play representativeness are not claimed.

Trace capture and ownership validation still exceed a 25 ms whole-decision
budget on tested branching positions: the native author stress checks measured
approximately 306 ms for H_v1 and 259 ms for H8 relay. Those checks establish
engineering behavior, not equal-time strength. Production evaluation needs a
separately admitted schedule and an adequate timing implementation.

No new teacher labels, optimizer steps, optimizer draws or production arena
games were authorized or performed by this stage. These remaining admission
limits are explicit conditions of the accepted engineering scope.
