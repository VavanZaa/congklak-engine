# Independent audit: deeper teachers and relay features

The final audit passed. All 13 independent checks, eight trained models, 640 checkpoint states, 391 position cases and 288 terminal games were verified. The H8 relay representative passed the predeclared ordinary-value and primary development seed-margin gates. H_v1 remains the default under the plan's no-automatic-promotion rule.

The experiment preserves the corrected corpus's 8,000 training and 1,000 development roots. H6 and H8 values are distinct targets. A root's complete H8 action scores have successor horizon H7; they do not become successor training examples. No sealed target bodies or oracle labels enter training or model selection.

The value-only factorial uses two seeds for each of four conditions: H6 or H8 teachers with 43 count inputs or 127 count-plus-relay inputs. Every model uses width 64, 8,000 optimizer steps, batch size 512 and 100-step checkpoint opportunities. The unused policy head is frozen and excluded from the optimizer. Relay models have more parameters and perform extra simulated moves, so equal optimizer steps do not mean equal training work or inference cost.

The original V1 teacher could reject its own saved labels on resume because the outer analyze call sampled elapsed time after the completed report. The independent recovery fixture reproduced that mismatch. A versioned V2 migration preserves V1 and changes only the label's elapsed-work snapshot to match its original report. The original outer elapsed values and before/after row digests remain in migration provenance. An exhaustive independent comparison verified 9,000 root bodies, 3,473 feature bodies, and all 3,472 original reports, targets and action scores. Exactly 543 rows changed timing metadata: 536 by one millisecond and seven by two milliseconds. An isolated full-function regression accepted and preserved an injected positive timing delta, and refused checksum-valid changes to other work counters, scores and generation identity.

The independent checks use a separate seed-by-seed rules interpreter for relay features, H8/H7 recurrence, selected-model forwards and minimax. All 9,000 published relay tensors were independently derived; 15,020 candidate teacher PVs were replayed to their H_v1 horizon or terminal leaves. H8 changed the H6 root value on 7,580 roots and the best-move set on 2,575 roots. Every one of the eight models was checked on all 1,000 development roots. All 640 tensor checkpoints were safely loaded and checked, including frozen dummy heads, six-tensor optimizer ownership, latest/best model digests and all RNG fields. Each seed's complete 4,096,000 stratum/row draws was independently replayed and matched all four conditions.

The 13 unique checks were collected from source-bound groups: two CUDA checks before production, one case-recovery check before timing, and ten core/migration/arena checks afterward. There were no duplicate methods or skipped checks. Real analyzer and teacher processes were killed at commit boundaries. A CUDA process was killed after an uncommitted step-20 file; it resumed from committed step 10 and matched fresh model, optimizer, sampler, Python RNG, Torch CPU/CUDA RNG and best-model state bit for bit. A second CUDA kill after the final checkpoint but before export regenerated the exact fresh selected export without another optimizer or selection step. Completed model, result, catalog and checkpoint bytes and modification times stayed unchanged. Checksum-valid forged exports, metrics, features and work counters were refused.

The benchmark resumed after an actual case-commit kill without changing the prior case. Two further process kills on an isolated production backup preserved the first completed arena game and recovered the second; both were literally replayed, idempotent validation passed, and a forged margin was refused. The production 288-game ledger was untouched. All final 391 cases and 288 games were independently checked; summaries, paired margins, family bootstrap intervals and gates were recomputed. The H-only relay adapter matched H_v1 values and best moves on all 765 comparisons where their completed horizons were equal. CPU affinity was restored after both normal and exceptional exits.

The predeclared strength protocol has 391 roots and 288 games: 100 original H6 proxy roots, 32 known exact diagnostics with 16 sealed aliases excluded, and 259 ordinary development roots referenced by H8. Seven engines receive equal 25/100 ms native-search deadlines on the same declared Windows performance core; relay feature work is charged to the search. Whole validated-call wall times are reported separately. Four arenas each use 36 fresh, contested development families and both seats at 20 ms per choice. The primary measure is paired final seed margin with a family bootstrap; a positive 95% lower bound and zero fallback choices are required for a strength gate. Every learned representative also requires the ordinary H8 no-regression gate.

All games reached terminal boards without fallback moves. The H8 relay representative won 36, drew one and lost 35 against H_v1; its WDL advantage is descriptive. Both relay representatives passed all three eligible ordinary H8 value strata; both count representatives failed. The evidence supports the tested H8-plus-relay combination's development seed-margin gate. The direct relay-vs-count interval includes zero, and deeper count targets alone did not improve the tested arena result. At both root-analysis budgets, H_v1 retained better H8 proxy agreement and regret. Such finite-horizon proxy comparisons can differ from full-game outcomes and are not an optimal-play verdict. Intervals are per comparison on development families, with no sealed target bodies consumed. No full-game solution, broad superiority or lower-power claim is established.

Diagnostic failures remain preserved. An early case fixture correctly refused a resume while the parent intentionally changed reporting-source wording before production freeze; the fresh frozen-source fixture passed. The first final-audit attempt used the requested option name `avx2` where frozen native bindings report `avx2-double-packed`; only that audit expectation was corrected. Production search, models, sources and outcomes were unchanged, and the full audit rerun passed.
