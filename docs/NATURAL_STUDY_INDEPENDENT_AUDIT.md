## Verified paired findings and limits

Every production root had both complete references, so the production paired denominator is 36 with no completion censoring. A synthetic mixed complete/unpaired summary separately verified that unpaired null metrics do not enter means. Every comparative field, status count and grouped mean was independently rederived with actor-correct signs and the same fixed prediction at both horizons.

The best-move sets became disjoint on six of 36 roots: four fresh roots and two old follow-ups. The lexicographic H8-best choice remained H10-best on 30 roots. Mean absolute root-target shift was 3.028 seeds; mean within-root absolute action-Q shift was 4.038 seeds. Mean H10 regret of the lexicographic H8-best action was 0.472 seeds. These are differences between two approximate teacher horizons, not proven game-theoretic errors.
