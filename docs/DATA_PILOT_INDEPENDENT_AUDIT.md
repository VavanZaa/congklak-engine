# Ordinary-position data pilot independent audit

Status: all eight independent methods passed, alongside four author checks. The completed data pilot contains 62 complete H8 labels. No model training, sampler application, pruning or playing-strength claim was performed.

The fixed model is `data/quality-train-v1/model.json` (SHA-256 `bc1160e99f419b3c1052ea87b28145b622639e6e76a49127c388ab4926d67558`). The new baseline manifest protects 1,834 files: all 1,829 previously accepted inputs, the preceding acceptance itself, and the completed control-diagnosis evidence. Existing sources, Betawi rules, model exports and accepted results remain unchanged.

The bounded collection contains 256 fresh standard-opening history families: 128 uniformly random families and 128 epsilon-0.25 fixed-policy families after eight random opening choices. Each family has at most 192 choices. Family train-candidate/validation ownership is assigned by a fixed hash before history generation. Validation is consumed development validation, not an unseen or sealed release test.

Only strict ordinary roots with 25–98 active seeds are candidates. The unchanged classifier examines every legal root move: any capture, own-store bonus or forced pass makes the root nonordinary. Terminal successors follow that existing classifier and are counted explicitly. The full bounded pool is collected before selection. Cross-cohort duplicate candidate quotients are excluded from both cohorts; selection is deterministic and allows at most one root per family and band.

Protection includes the inherited registry, all original 9,000 root identities and the complete trajectories of all 192 preceding value arena games. Protected candidate-root quotients are excluded from fresh train-candidate and validation sets. Shared standard-opening or other history prefixes do not reject entire families; this is root-level novelty with explicit family ownership, not disjoint complete state spaces. Existing arena roots remain separately owned diagnostics and never silently become training rows. Diagnostic selection is restricted to previously unlabeled ordinary roots with 25–98 active seeds.

The caps are 32 train-candidate and eight validation roots per 25–49 and 50–74 band, plus exploratory caps of eight and two in 75–98, and up to 16 saved diagnostic roots: at most 106 labeling attempts. There is no filler, expansion of the family budget or scarcity-driven quota change. Yield denominators, duplicate/protected exclusions and label rejections must remain visible.

The teacher is the accepted H_v1 search at eight choices, including bonus choices, with a 2,000,000-node bound, no time cutoff, 16 MiB TT and PVS. Every accepted label needs the complete legal action set and validated exact finite-horizon action values. This is an approximate H8 evaluation, not an exact eventual-game proof. Bounded partial results are retained as rejected attempts and cannot count as completed labels or be converted into targets.

Independent verification replayed all fresh production histories and all 192 protected arena trajectories with the seed-by-seed rules interpreter. It reconstructed ownership, collision closure and sampling arithmetic, validated every accepted teacher report and literal principal variation, and verified source/model immutability. Separate fixture directories exercised real collection and labeling process kills before and after commits, prefix retention, idempotent completion, incompatible identities and malformed/partial label refusal. Raw workers, markers and journals remain retained under `build/data-pilot-audit/`.

The protection reference covered all 9,000 old root identities, 192 old games and 6,461 old choices. The resulting 42,824 protected quotients and eight novel diagnostic roots matched the producer's registry exactly. Old sealed target bodies were not read. The diagnostic roots belong to five existing families; two roots have constructed-start family provenance. They remain separately owned evaluation evidence.

None occurred in 75–98. All 87 observations had distinct quotients; none hit the protected set or crossed cohort ownership. Selection preserved the caps and the one-root-per-family/band rule.
