# Ordinary value pilot: overlap, H_v1 margins and H10 errors

**Keep the compact control and its policy fixed. The previous HOLD stays in force; this investigation starts no training, labeling search, strength games or self-play.** H_v1 remains the default analyzer. The findings explain the recorded arithmetic and narrow the next diagnostic; they do not establish a unique cause of a timed-game loss or justify large training.

## The overlap hold was applied correctly

The original gate compared arena trajectories with every admitted optimizer row, not only rows actually sampled. Rebuilding all 7,587 admitted rows and 3,268 saved decisions reproduces two observations sharing one five-active-seed quotient. There are no admitted exact raw-state matches, full-state matches after player swap, family matches or currently sampled-row matches. The matching rehearsal row had zero draws in the 200-step value pilot.

This is nevertheless a meaningful transformed state: swapping players aligns the pits and actor; aligned training stores `[48,45]` differ from arena stores `[53,40]` by `[+5,-5]`. All 127 actor-relative neural inputs are identical; the exact player0 store-difference offset changes by ten seeds. We did not transfer its H8 label or action values into the arena analysis.

The independent auditor found an already-sealed ancestry link: the matching row belonged to the control's earlier admitted teacher set, and the accepted old sampling review showed positive draws for all 7,669 roots in that set over 384,000 draws. Thus prior draw exposure is established even though current pilot draws are zero. Exact old per-row counts and nonzero gradient contributions are not reconstructed here. Both compared models inherit that exposure; it does not identify a cause of their difference.

The two overlap observations occur in the same matched pair0/seat0 trajectory: both models win by 18 seeds in 45 choices. They are outside the three adverse H_v1 margin changes. This is descriptive separation, not a causal proof. Ignoring only the admitted-but-undrawn match would hypothetically select `repeat_high`, not fresh-start expansion, because the candidate's validation error and H_v1 mean gain still fail the favorable conditions. No gate, old report, role ledger or allocation was changed. All 96 latest full trajectories are already protected for future work.

These total -16 seeds over 24 games, giving the reported -2/3-seed mean change. All three start in capture-stratum families; the actual divergent positions are respectively `5-24/capture`, `25-49/capture` and `50-74/bonus`. Start mechanism and divergence mechanism are separate facts. The two other changed trajectories retain their margins. Dropping any one family leaves the H_v1 mean change negative (-0.7273 to -0.4545), a descriptive sensitivity check rather than new independent evidence.

Root priors match at the learned-engine divergences, as expected from fixed policy. Saved value estimates, Q, visits and completed simulations differ. The timed protocol therefore does not isolate the changed value weights from differences in search work. Local store gains and captures describe the paths but cannot prove which first move was wrong. The ten first-divergence events across both baselines occupy nine unique full states; none has an exact-full-state Q match in the declared consumed-reference index. That index contains 17,979 references over 9,178 states; absence there is not a claim of absence in every possible archive.

H8 relay context also has 19/24 identical trajectories. Its three changed seat margins sum to zero; two first divergences belong to the opponent with different completed depths. No new arena or confidence interval was generated.

## The value update is small, but target support is weak

The six protected requested-H10 roots retain the original primary reference. MAE changes from 9.599085 to 9.632052 seeds: three roots improve and three worsen. Low-band mean error improves by 0.039749 seeds; high-band error worsens by 0.105682. All three high-band roots have player1 to move, so this panel confounds actor and phase. The target interval contains all six targets strictly; a hard tanh output bound is not the explanation. This does not prove simultaneous fit by the fixed hidden representation.

The head-shift algebra shows small bias change and larger hidden-weight contributions, with the store offset unchanged. The predeclared mathematical interpolation grid `{0,.25,.5,.75,1}` yields H10 MAEs 9.5991, 9.6073, 9.6156, 9.6238 and 9.6321. That diagnostic shows no benefit along this particular update direction on these six roots; it selects no model, checkpoint, learning rate or optimizer setting. No interpolated model was exported.

Using the same fixed predictions against H12 gives 12.0493 to 12.1605 seeds. One target shifts by eleven seeds between H10 and H12, and one root changes best move. Deeper finite targets are sensitivity evidence, not full-game truth; H10 was not replaced after results.

Actual exposure reconstructs exactly: 3,200 high, 3,200 low and 6,400 rehearsal draws. The 6,400 self-play draws come from only 24 raw start states and two individual returns each. Returns disagree at 22/24 starts, with median gap nine and maximum 56 seeds. The rehearsal half samples 4,318 unique roots from 7,539 admitted roots, all of which had positive ancestral draw exposure. Repeated draws are not independent data, and observed control-policy returns and approximate minimax-style H8 targets estimate different quantities. These facts support inspecting target reliability and mixture effects, not attributing the losses to a particular weight or automatically increasing games.

Five target-blind actor-relative pit-distance neighbors were selected per validation root, with tie by row ID. Their distances range from 10 to 33 seeds. This is a coverage description, not evidence that nearby boards share value. The validation review uses at most 60 unique states for literal fixed-model calculations; the arena review uses 18 forwards at nine unique divergence states. Global frozen policy/trunk identity is verified. No Torch or optimizer runtime was imported.

## What should happen before another pilot

1. Compare control and candidate at fixed simulation counts on every adverse H_v1 shared-prefix decision, with unchanged-margin divergence controls. Keep policy and models fixed and use the same search configuration. This separates a controlled diagnostic from timed strength evidence.
2. Where exact-state Q is absent, predeclare a small deeper per-action teacher budget and retain incomplete/horizon-sensitive results. Check whether the difficult ordinary targets stabilize before using them for learning.
3. Prospectively distinguish ancestral exposure, newly sampled rows, admitted-only rows and raw/transformed-state overlap. Keep the deliberate encoder equivalence and all existing protection masks; never revise the old hold or call consumed development unseen.
4. Allocate more distinct starts where coverage is weak and more independent rollouts where outcome uncertainty remains high. Preserve capture/endgame rehearsal and separate target estimands before considering another small value-only experiment. Large training is premature.
