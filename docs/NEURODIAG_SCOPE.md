# Existing-training-source neural diagnostics

This versioned study inspects target consistency and fixed-feature value fit using existing eligible training sources. The control is `data/quality-train-v1/model.json` (SHA-256 `bc1160e99f419b3c1052ea87b28145b622639e6e76a49127c388ab4926d67558`); the candidate is the unchanged `data/ordinary-value-pilot-v1/model.json`. Their six shared trunk/policy tensors must remain byte-identical. No model, policy, training loss, sampler, optimizer, label, game, search budget or promotion decision changes. The baseline `build/neurodiag-baseline.json` protects 5,377 prior files.

This is an **in-sample training-source diagnostic**, not a new assessment partition. Historical training roles remain explicit. Examining these rows creates no new future optimizer reservation; all existing protected/reserved overlays continue to apply. Findings cannot establish generalization, playing strength, a causal training remedy or a population-wide capacity limit. Saved protection covers known roots and trajectories, not every possible future trajectory or ancestral independence.

## Numerical questions and limits

Fixed FP32 export weights are evaluated with unrounded cached features in float64. This is a declared scalar-export diagnostic, not a claim of bit parity with historical Torch FP32 execution. Outputs preserve player-0 seed units `y=d+r*tanh(u)`, with signed active seeds `r` negative for actor 1. Normalized targets are `z=(target-d)/r`. Physical admissibility includes `[-1,1]`; finite-head exact representability is the open interval `(-1,1)`. Exact endpoints remain counted and are excluded from finite `atanh` projections rather than clipped.

The frozen helper records policy identity, hidden/ReLU activity, saturation and near-kink flags, scalar errors, normalized Huber(delta `.1`) diagnostics, analytic output/loss Jacobians and gradient cancellation at these fixed parameters. Feature derivatives are local continuous encoded-feature sensitivities, not causal effects of legal board changes. H8/H10 estimates and observed terminal returns are separate estimands; their risks and projections are never pooled into a single truth target.

Per `(cohort,target_type)` summaries explicitly distinguish root-balanced and family-balanced mass, individual record counts and inverse-concentration support. These quantities are not independent sample counts, uncertainty or an empirical noise floor. Cached-feature/hidden geometry and weighted float64 SVD use fixed `rcond=1e-10`. Strict-interior target projection uses `[hidden64,1]` against `atanh(z)`. It measures in-sample preactivation representability and may be trivial with at most 65 independent interior rows; it is not a seed-error capacity lower bound. No coefficients are exported, fitted model selected or parameters applied. Numerical settings, version/thread/backend identity and all fixed diagnostic thresholds are frozen in `NEURODIAG_METRICS.md` and helper `SETTINGS` before observations. NumPy `2.3.2`, threadpoolctl `3.6.0`, one active numerical thread are required.

## Durable phases, certification and recovery

`data/neurodiag-v1/work.sqlite3` uses single-writer SQLite FULL/WAL transactions. Immutable frozen inputs precede generation. Each logical batch contains 16 units (last production batch may be shorter); observation rows and its complete envelope commit together. Completed prefixes validate hashes/source identities and only unfinished batches may call `observe`. Telemetry stays outside semantic cache payloads; cached observations and summaries contain no timing, PID or nonce fields.

After all observations, the full numerical/SVD summary is computed once and committed with the complete marker before DB finalization. Required `summary.json` and `manifest.json` publish from this validated cache. The resulting cache is explicitly `complete_uncertified`. The audit-only `raw_complete` reader exposes it without claiming model conformance. An incomplete publication may repair these raw exports only while uncertified. Required certified cache files are not repairable exports.

Independent production methods 04/05 verify actual source targets, scalar forwards, policy identity, analytic derivatives and cached numerical/SVD summaries, then emit the external witness `build/neurodiag-audit/observation-witness.json`. The witness pins the completed DB, manifest, summary, row hashes, models, inputs and sources. It does not pin its future production group JSON. The production group pins the witness; neither process writes certification state into the immutable DB or manifest. Public completed APIs require both current passing production2 and the witness. They validate cached sources/rows and return the persisted summary without any encoder, forward, derivative, SVD or summary recomputation. Missing required cache/certificate evidence refuses. A missing final `build/neurodiag-result.json` alone can be repaired from valid certified data.

Source-frozen engineering fixtures use the first 48 frozen units in three batches of 16. Real independent kills target the second batch: precommit retains prefix one/16 units; postcommit retains prefix two/32 units. Exact fresh/resumed cached observations, summary and retained-prefix bytes must agree, and the observation-call ledger must show no repeated committed units. These engineering forwards are disclosed and do not establish independent scientific outcomes.

Four data-owner methods, four helper-owner methods and six independent methods are planned, with source-bound execution proofs and retained failures if any. Root is sole production launcher after owner8 and independent preflight3. No Native construction/transition/analysis, Torch, optimizer, teacher or PUCT search, new game or new label is authorized at any stage. Completed reruns add no observations, commits, forwards, derivatives or SVD and preserve durable bytes/size/mtime. Pure source/hash/structural validation may repeat.
