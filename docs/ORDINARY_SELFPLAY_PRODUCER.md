# Ordinary coverage data producer

The fixed quality control model generates a predeclared512-prefix legal-history pool, seed20261026. Half the prefixes choose random legal moves; half use the frozen policy with epsilon0.25 after eight random opening choices. Each prefix stops below25 active seeds or after192 choices. These are history prefixes, not necessarily complete games. Ownership uses `sha256(ordinary-selfplay-owner-v1/+family)` before generation, with training below80 modulo100. Family IDs are `ordinary-selfplay-history/seed/index`.

Only strict ordinary roots with at least two legal moves and25–74 active seeds are collection candidates. The unchanged rule helper tests every legal continuation. The entire pool enters transitive eligible-quotient/family closure before quotas; protected or mixed-ownership components are excluded whole. Deterministic matching selects at most12 high and12 low training roots, plus three validation roots per band, all in distinct families. Shortfalls remain visible without filler or expansion.

Collection novelty protects prior metadata roots, previous generated family IDs and self-play observations. The separate rollout evaluation mask preserves actual prior evaluation ownership and every state, including terminal states, in the latest96 evaluation trajectories. Prior training metadata does not become a blanket rollout evaluation ban. Before rollouts, the closed pool adds every new validation-owned family's eligible candidate quotients and family ID to protection, including candidates outside the selected six-root panel. This ledger is published immutably and bound by the rollout identity with the closed collection manifest. The shared database may grow during rollouts; its collection tables and publications remain unchanged.

Each selected training root produces two fixed-control trajectories, at most48 games. PUCT uses64 simulations, c0.25,2048 nodes, root Dirichlet alpha0.3/fraction0.25, temperature1 for eight choices then0, and at most512 choices. Full games retain every mechanism through the terminal result. Candidate optimizer rows require strict ordinary, at least25 active seeds and at least two legal actions. A protected eligible ordinary bridge rejects the entire family. Incomplete pairs have no manufactured returns. Raw policy targets remain visits/64 regardless of behavior temperature; values are actual fixed-player0 terminal margins with bonus-turn orientation preserved.

Keep at most four observations per raw state, with choice0 first and then deterministic `sha256(ordinary-selfplay-rawcap-v1/+rowid)` order. A usable start requires both terminal choice0 policy/return records retained. Count distinct starts and families separately from descendants and duplicate observations. The coverage gate requires at least eight usable high and eight usable low starts. A complete three-plus-three validation panel is a separate future-use requirement. This phase performs no optimizer work, teacher searches, strength games or model promotion.

The new namespace is `data/ordinary-selfplay-pilot-v1`. `collect()` and `generate()` commit one family or decision at a time in SQLite WAL/FULL under one writer lock. `load_rows()` returns `(roots,collection_manifest)`; `load_replay()` returns `(replay_manifest,rows)`. Completed readers validate source identities, saved legal histories, selection closure, rules, raw visits, RNG chains, terminal returns and admission before accepting or repairing publications. Reruns perform no new PUCT search, Torch activation or writes to durable completed files; native policy forwards may revalidate saved histories.

Build-only collection fixtures use the predetermined first32 history families and one selected root per cohort/band, with honest shortfalls. Their rollout panel is explicitly separate: lexicographically first accepted high ordinary training root, then the first distinct-family low ordinary training root from the prior39-root metadata set, with exact source hashes. This is declared before fixture outcomes, uses four simulations and never supplies production fallback roots. Actual collection and decision pre/postcommit barriers support process-kill recovery checks.
