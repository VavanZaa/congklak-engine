# Research basis for a fixed-mixture weighting assessment

The proposed change is to normalize the existing stability heuristic inside each stratum-by-actor cell. It preserves a declared assessment mixture while testing whether weighting concentrates on lower finite-horizon decision regret. It does not estimate natural gameplay frequency, calibrate uncertainty, train a model or establish stronger play.

Gelman's treatment of poststratification separates cell estimates from the fixed masses used to combine them. Its population interpretation requires the relevant population sizes and sampling assumptions. Our application uses the same weighted-sum structure with a deliberately chosen mixture: four strata at 25% each and two actors within each stratum at 50% each. Those are experimental targets, not measured population proportions. Keeping these masses fixed prevents a favorable pooled result obtained merely by moving probability away from an unfavorable group. [Gelman, *Struggles with Survey Weighting and Regression Modeling* (2007)](https://sites.stat.columbia.edu/gelman/research/published/STS226.pdf)

Importance weighting under covariate shift has a different justification: it uses the target-to-source input-density ratio, with appropriate support and unchanged conditional outcome assumptions. Our H8/H10 stability heuristic is not such a ratio. A normalized heuristic therefore changes the within-cell assessment objective; it cannot be described as unbiased correction for a known deployment distribution. [Sugiyama, Krauledat and Müller, *Covariate Shift Adaptation by Importance Weighted Cross Validation* (2007)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v8/sugiyama07a.html)

Group DRO explicitly targets worst-group risk; the original neural-network study also shows that favorable training-group fit need not give favorable worst-group generalization. We borrow the evaluation lesson, rather than its training algorithm: expose every stratum and actor cell, and require ordinary-group decision regret not to worsen even if the pooled mean improves. With only two roots per cell, these are descriptive safeguards on selected data, not generalization guarantees. [Sagawa et al., *Distributionally Robust Neural Networks for Group Shifts* (2019/2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08731)

Decision-focused work distinguishes prediction error from the quality of the decision induced by that prediction. This motivates making full H10-best-set regret against the requested H12 reference primary, while reporting root-value and full-action-Q shifts separately. We are not implementing SPO training or claiming its optimization guarantees apply to PUCT. The H12 target remains a bounded heuristic reference. [Elmachtoub and Grigas, *Smart "Predict, then Optimize"*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.08005), [Donti, Amos and Kolter, *Task-based End-to-end Model Learning in Stochastic Optimization* (2017)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/3fc2c60b5782f641f76bcefc39fb2392-Abstract.html)

Pareto-smoothed importance sampling addresses unstable importance ratios and provides diagnostics for that setting. There are neither estimated density ratios nor enough observations per cell for a useful tail fit here. We retain the existing bounded heuristic, exact probabilities, maximum exposure and inverse-squared-probability effective support. That support describes concentration only; it is not a count of independent games, an uncertainty interval or optimizer-gradient ESS. No PSIS fit or parameter sweep is proposed. [Vehtari et al., *Pareto Smoothed Importance Sampling* (2024)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v25/19-556.html)

The design must have separate eligible support. The remaining already-labeled pool after the preceding reservations has 37 roots: 34 high ordinary, three low ordinary, no bonus and no capture. It cannot fill the four-stratum panel. Blind inventory of other saved raw support must establish two distinct components and origin families in each of eight cells before selection freezes. One root per cell would force its probability to 1/8 whatever its heuristic score, making a within-cell weighting comparison trivial. Empty cells cannot be silently omitted or have their mass assigned elsewhere.

Freeze the 16 raw roots and formula before current H8/H10 attempts; existing historical H8/H10 exposure remains allowed and explicitly reported. Freeze the new exact endpoint-linked rational weights before H12. Reuse the single existing raw rule without fitting its scales or floor. Both comparators retain the same eight cell masses. Missing requested references retain their original probability mass and block a favorable screen; auxiliary horizons cannot substitute. Uniform and weighted full-tie regret, root/Q shifts, concentration, missing mass and each ordinary cell remain visible regardless of the result. No current sampling or loss weights are applied. If a future sampler draws according to these probabilities, unit loss already expresses that objective; applying the same weighting again would change it a second time.
