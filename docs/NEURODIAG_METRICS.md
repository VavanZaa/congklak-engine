# Fixed-export neural diagnostics

This helper inspects the two existing control and ordinary-value exports without changing parameters, training, searches, games, labels or model files. It uses existing admitted targets and features. The data producer owns the frozen panel and its ownership exclusions. Numerical findings describe retrospective eligible training-source diagnostic support, not a new development/assessment partition, evidence of playing strength or a proposed training solution.

`congklak/neurodiag_metrics.py` exposes `load_models()`, `literal_features(state)`, `observe(units, models)`, `validate_cached(batch, units=None, model_identity=None)` and `summarize_cached(batches)`. All units are keyed by the canonical digest of their exact raw state key and `{x,d,r}` features. Repeated scalar targets remain separate records associated with one forward unit. No player-swap or quotient target reuse occurs.

An observation batch contains `schema`, `source_units_sha256`, `models`, `runtime`, `observations` and `counts`. Each observation retains its complete source unit and checksum, cached preactivations, hidden activations, positive ReLU masks, near-kink flags, policy logits/probabilities, two value-head observations and individual target diagnostics. There are no newly measured timing fields. Counts distinguish one shared trunk and policy evaluation per unit from two value-head/model-view evaluations. Replaying a cached observation does not count as a new forward.

The intended panel has 256 base H8 roots, 28 H8/H10 source positions represented by 56 scalar targets, and 48 individual terminal-return records from 24 paired self-play starts: 332 source positions, 360 target records and at most 308 distinct forward units. Shared raw states and identical features share a forward. The primary global 256-root group remains explicit; small per-cell or paired groups are not a substitute for that global feature analysis. The production data contract, including exact final counts and source exposure, is in `NEURODIAG_SCOPE.md`.

## Numerical contract

The helper verifies export checksums, architecture and all eight FP32 tensor payloads. The control and candidate must have identical six trunk/policy tensors. Stored weights are rounded to FP32 as in the accepted export loader, then evaluated in float64 with **unrounded cached float64 features**. This is the frozen scalar-export diagnostic contract, not bit parity with the historical Torch FP32 training/cache execution.

For each nonterminal unit, the shared trunk computes two affine/ReLU layers and policy logits once. Each fixed value head computes preactivation `u`, `t=tanh(u)` and fixed-player0 seed value `y=d+r*t`; `r` is signed active seeds, negative for actor 1. Policy probabilities mask illegal pits and use the same shared logits. The scalar conformance test compiles only the exact accepted `Model.reference` and rounding AST with an encoded-feature stub; it never imports or constructs Native. Terminal scalar conformance preserves the accepted early return `(d,256*d,[0]*7)`; production observation units are nonterminal and reject `r=0`.

The declared model saturation threshold is `abs(t)>=.95`; target near-boundary is `abs(z)>=.95`, with `z=(target-d)/r`. Exact endpoint targets `z=±1` are retained and counted separately. Targets outside `[-1,1]` refuse admission; there is no clipping. Analytic derivatives retain `dy/du=r*(1-t*t)`. Normalized-output and seed-output Jacobians and norms are separate. Zero ReLU preactivations use derivative zero; `abs(preactivation)<=1e-8` is explicitly flagged as near a kink. Finite differences use scratch synthetic weights/inputs away from kinks, step `1e-6`, absolute tolerance `1e-6` and relative tolerance `1e-4`. Scalar output/logit parity uses absolute tolerance `1e-10` and relative tolerance `1e-12`.

Per-target diagnostics include seed error, normalized residual error, and unreduced normalized-residual Huber loss with delta `.1`, derivative `clip(t-z,-.1,.1)`. Fixed-head loss-gradient cancellation is `norm(sum(p*g))/sum(p*norm(g))`, with a null ratio for a zero denominator. This is a descriptive fixed-parameter gradient diagnostic. It is neither a reconstruction of historical training gradients nor the derivative of seed MAE. Input Jacobians describe local continuous **encoded-feature** sensitivity, not derivatives of legal discrete boards or causal importance of capture/bonus features. Encoded features are correlated and include discontinuous binary and relay-derived quantities.

`literal_features` is a pure implementation of the accepted 43+84 relay-feature specification using `tests/reference.py`. It performs hypothetical literal sowing only, never Native, inference or label generation. The sole production generation allowance is the 13 admitted paired roots lacking an existing cache. Fifteen cached paired vectors are independently compared with this specification. Base and self-play feature caches remain source-bound. Owner checks use only three synthetic engineering states, not the selected production panel.

## Weights and projection

Each `(cohort,target_type)` has separate raw-root-balanced and family-balanced summaries; different target estimands are never pooled into one risk or projection. Raw-root balancing gives equal mass to each exact raw root and splits that root's mass among its scalar records. Family balancing gives equal family mass, equal root mass within a family, then equal record mass at that root. Subgroup risks are explicitly conditional on their own stated support. Concentration `1/sum(p*p)` is reported at record/root/family levels; it is not independent sample count, importance-sampling ESS, confidence or effective games.

For strict-interior targets only, the SVD target is `atanh(z)` and the design is cached `[hidden64,1]`. Original selected, interior and endpoint mass is retained before conditioning the interior projection. The weighted matrix is `sqrt(p)*A`, and the weighted logit target is `sqrt(p)*atanh(z)`. Float64 SVD uses fixed `rcond=1e-10`; retained sample-space columns `U` yield residual `b-U*(U.T*b)`. The helper never constructs affine head coefficients, exports a candidate, or applies an optimizer. The residual describes **in-sample preactivation/logit-space representability**. It is not a seed-error capacity lower bound: tanh outputs do not generally lie in the linear span of hidden features. Full row rank at at most 65 interior records is explicitly flagged as a possible trivial interpolation result. Endpoint targets have no finite artanh value and are never silently clipped.

Feature and hidden geometry use weighted centered matrices, with variance, zero-variance columns, singular values, rank and condition number on the retained identified subspace. Conditioning is descriptive of this fixed support. Shared-layer activity and near-kink counts use distinct forward units. Paired H8/H10 target shifts remain separate finite-horizon estimands. Two observed returns at the same self-play start remain dependent outcomes; their empirical spread is not a calibrated noise floor or generalization bound.

NumPy is pinned to `2.3.2` and `threadpoolctl` to `3.6.0`. BLAS/OpenMP environment variables are assigned exactly `1` before the first helper NumPy import and active numerical pools are limited to one thread. Runtime metadata records Python/library versions, numerical settings and backend-library hashes without paths, clocks or process IDs.

## Cache, certification and checks

`observe` is called only for a new batch. The producer atomically commits the whole logical batch envelope and normalized observation rows. `summarize_cached` computes the full numerical/SVD summary once before the complete marker. Independent production checks rederive all numerical results and issue an external observation witness binding the immutable DB, manifest, summary, model sources and freezes. That witness does not create a hash cycle by pinning its future group report. The completed public reader requires both that certificate and current passing production proof.

`validate_cached` checks structure, source checksums, exact count types and elementary cached error arithmetic only. It does not call the encoder, model parser, forward, gradient, NumPy or SVD. Numerical truth is established by the external independent certificate, rather than by disguising a new forward as validation. **The public completed API/CLI returns the certified persisted summary and must not call even `summarize_cached` or any SVD routine.** Pure structural admission and hash validation may repeat; no new observation or numerical reconstruction is implied.

The four source-frozen owner methods in `tests/neurodiag_metrics_author.py` check: literal features and exact accepted scalar AST/export/policy/terminal conformance; actor-signed analytic derivatives against central differences and Huber/kink semantics; exact root/family masses, endpoint exclusion, known-span/orthogonal/conflicting-target SVD cases; and cache-only admission, source/counter corruption refusal and byte/mtime-stable synthetic summary rebuild. Freeze is `build/neurodiag-metrics-implementation.json`; source-bound actual execution is `build/neurodiag-metrics-author.json`. Formal fixtures must not run before the freeze. Retained owner fixtures are synthetic and do not constitute production diagnostics or additional independent methods.
