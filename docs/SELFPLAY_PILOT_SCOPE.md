# Current-model self-play training pilot

This is one bounded engineering and strength experiment for Betawi `id-betawi-relay7-v0`, using final seed margin as its payoff. The accepted control is `data/quality-train-v1/model.json` (file SHA256 `bc1160e99f419b3c1052ea87b28145b622639e6e76a49127c388ab4926d67558`). The default remains H_v1. The rejected value-only pilot is not the starting network.

## Frozen experimental outline

- Start from 16 distinct eligible fresh midgame training families, one declared start per family and two seeded trajectories per start: at most 32 games, 512 pit choices per game. Four starts each come from 50-74 active ordinary, 25-49 ordinary, 25-49 bonus and 25-49 capture strata, selected by deterministic family-to-quota matching before generation. This is midgame-start self-play, not a full-start game-distribution estimate. No replacement families after observing outcomes.
- Use the existing compact 127-input, width-64, 12,872-parameter network and improved CPU PUCT: fixed-player-zero values, actor-dependent selection, parent-bootstrap FPU, c_puct 0.25, 64 completed simulations per decision, and at most 2,048 tree nodes. Generation uses root Dirichlet alpha 0.3 mixed at fraction 0.25; behavior samples the visit distribution at temperature 1 for the first eight choices, then uses maximum visits at temperature 0. The training policy remains visits divided by 64. Fixed simulation counts permit deterministic generation recovery. Evaluation has no exploration noise.
- Record root visit policies and exact completed-game final margins. Optimizer-eligible roots are nonterminal with at least 25 active seeds, defined before protection checks. Full episodes still continue through the late game, exact endgame and terminal result. All positions below 25 active seeds are excluded from this targeted midgame optimizer domain; report the exact-domain and other late exclusions separately. Admit a family only when both trajectories terminate, and reject protected ownership collisions on its eligible roots. Capped games have no invented draw or value labels. Keep excluded games and reasons visible. Retain at most four observations per raw state globally using deterministic hash order, without averaging returns. Draw uniformly from admitted families, then uniformly from that family's retained rows; publish both expected and reconstructed committed exposure.
- Require at least eight admitted families with retained eligible rows before production training. A smaller yield finishes and reports the recoverable data collection but blocks optimization; it does not trigger replacement families or an expanded budget.
- Train one joint policy/value challenger for 200 deterministic CPU steps, batch 128, fresh AdamW at 0.0001, weight decay 0.0001, gradient clip 1, foreach disabled, one CPU thread, seed 20261025, checkpoints every 20 steps. Loss is residual-space Huber(delta 0.1) plus 0.25 times legal visit-policy cross entropy. Residual target is `(terminal_player0 - D_player0) / signed_active`; seed-space errors are descriptive. Start from the exact control weights and select the fixed final step. All eight weight tensors are trainable. This updates shared features, policy and value; it is a new experiment after the earlier fixed-policy studies. No hyperparameter or checkpoint selection from arena results.
- Reserve evaluation families and roots before generation or optimization. Protect actual evaluation ownership, known evaluation trajectories, the latest ten midgame validation roots and nine diagnostic roots. Collection-only duplicate reservations are not silently reclassified as evaluation exclusions. The latest 39 fresh H10 labels provide potential starts only: no teacher labels or teacher rehearsal enter gradient training. All gradients use admitted self-play rows. Final manifests declare noise, temperature, seeds, loss, replay probabilities and source identities before production.
- Run 96 contemporaneously interleaved development games: 12 starting families, both candidate seats, both H_v1 and H8 relay CPU opponents, and both the unchanged control and challenger. Give every engine the same 25 ms whole-decision allowance on the declared performance core. Record all decisions, terminal outcomes, elapsed costs and fallbacks. Report paired family margins and uncertainty, with win/draw/loss secondary. The 32 known-exact endgame roots at 25/100 ms across four engines give 256 diagnostic measurements.
- This small, previously inspected development setting cannot establish release strength or justify automatic promotion. Retain the original control and default regardless of this pilot's point estimates.

## Recovery and evidence

Preflight narrowed the provisional optimizer domain before production. A four-simulation engineering fixture had 68 protected overlaps, all at 5-11 active seeds, excluding 14 of 16 families under the provisional greater-than-four rule. Independent read-only reconstruction confirmed no overlap at 25 or more active seeds and 326 such observations across the same 16 families. The final domain uses the already declared midgame boundary of 25: it removes late training rows, keeps the evaluation mask unchanged, and retains whole-family exclusion for eligible overlaps. Original sources, plans, documents and fixture evidence are preserved. No production outcome or strength result informed this revision, and production still must pass the eight-family yield gate without replacement.

Self-play commits complete decisions atomically, retaining position, search report and RNG state. An interrupted uncommitted search may be recomputed; committed choices must not change. Replay is published only from validated terminal records. Training recovery must restore network, optimizer, sampler and RNG state, with an atomic checkpoint catalog. Equal-time games resume from committed decisions rather than replaying them through search.

An independent agent audits ownership, targets, current-model identity, legal policies, bonus turns, budgets and metrics. Real subprocess kills around decision and checkpoint commits, plus a benchmark interruption, must preserve committed prefixes and deterministic training results. Complete reruns must produce zero new searches, games, optimizer steps or training-runtime activation, and preserve durable artifacts and all prior pins.

`build/selfplay-pilot-baseline.json` preserves 2,839 previously accepted inputs, including the last data pilot and architecture/source audit. New producers, tests, database directories and observer use new names. No source data pruning or historical evidence rewriting is authorized. The dashboard reports build, audit, generation, admission, training, comparisons and completion, including errors and remaining limitations. Dashboard verification is through its Python backend and listener, without browser access.

## What this does not finish

Repeated successful improvement cycles, broad ordinary-position coverage, representative full-start self-play, robust held-out release strength, value calibration, larger replay scaling, measured inference/tree optimization, user-facing analysis explanations and product packaging remain separate work. A recoverable completed pilot is evidence about the next step, not a solved Congklak engine.
