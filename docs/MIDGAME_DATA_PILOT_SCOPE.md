# Targeted midgame collection

This versioned collection uses 512 deterministic standard-start legal-history prefixes, seed 20261023: 256 random prefixes and 256 frozen improved-policy epsilon-0.25 prefixes after eight random opening choices. Each stops after 192 choices, below 25 active seeds, or at termination. The fixed control policy/model remains unchanged; no Torch, optimizer or teacher search is used by the collector.

Fresh eligibility is exactly `50-74/ordinary`, `25-49/ordinary`, `25-49/bonus` and `25-49/capture`, with at least two legal root actions. Mechanism classification uses the existing `pilot._mechanism` across every legal choice, including its pass/capture/bonus precedence. The strict ordinary definition is unchanged. Caps are 16 training candidates plus four validation roots for 50–74 ordinary, and eight plus two for each 25–49 stratum: at most 50 fresh roots. Selection keeps at most one root per family and target stratum and one per active quotient. Missing natural supply remains an explicit shortfall; no 75–98 quota, constructed filler or automatic expansion is allowed.

Family IDs and 80/20 hash ownership are assigned before generation. The entire pool is collected before selection. A bipartite graph joins every eligible candidate's family and active quotient. Whole transitive components are excluded if they touch a protected quotient, an old protected family, or mixed training/validation ownership. Same-cohort remaining quotients are deduplicated. Shared standard-opening prefixes outside declared eligibility do not enter this graph and are not claimed disjoint.

The role-aware registry preserves all 83 old curated roles (61 training candidates, 14 validation and eight diagnostics), all 256 prior ordinary-pilot and 512 natural-study family IDs, 259 base ordinary development roots, all inherited protection, all latest 44 evaluation cases and 12 starts, and every state including terminal states of all 144 latest pre/posttraining games. Historical training roles are never rewritten as validation; later evaluation collisions restrict future eligibility and record ancestry. Exclusion cannot remove prior model exposure.

All nine saved first-divergence states form a separate diagnostic-only panel, including eight previously uncovered states and the one covered case. Their literal pits, stores, actor, actual band/mechanism, pair/seat, chosen actions and old-Q provenance are preserved without orientation or store-offset transforms. They never enter fresh training candidates. The label producer handles that panel and the fresh roots under its own frozen budget and journal.

New paths are `data/midgame-data-pilot-v1` and `build/midgame-data-pilot-{registry,plan,implementation,result}.json`. `load_rows(path)` strictly validates a closed collection and returns `(fresh_rows, manifest)`. The manifest binds source/rules/model/registry, all family and root hashes, selection components and shortfalls. `read_families(path)` returns the committed pool; scientific consumers first validate the closed manifest. Registry diagnostics are in the `diagnostics` mapping.

The exclusive writer uses SQLite WAL/FULL. Each legal-history family is an atomic unit with precommit/postcommit barriers. Recovery reuses committed families and rederives them for compatibility; an unfinished family is recomputed. A completed rerun may perform read-only history and policy-forward validation, while adding no new collection or teacher work and preserving durable bytes/mtimes. A complete marker is checked against actual tables and identities before external publication. The 2,639 baseline inputs remain unchanged.
