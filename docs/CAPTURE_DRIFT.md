Each root drift is the mean absolute Q change over every legal action. Its full-panel contribution is its unchanged probability times that mean. An action contributes `(W-U)*abs(delta)/number_of_legal_actions`. Summing actions reproduces roots, cells, capture, non-capture and all16 totals. Best/non-best action contributions preserve the full legal-action denominator; conditional means within a subset are separate fields. Capture-conditional averages must not be mistaken for contributions to the 16-root global result.

The translation diagnostic preserves `D=mean(abs(delta))`, `C=abs(mean(delta))`, `S=D-C>=0` and `E=mean(abs(delta-mean(delta)))`. Exactly D=C+S. S measures signed cancellation, not ranking error; E is centered dispersion and generally is not an additive remainder to C. Actor-relative signs and best-reference gap changes are separate: for an H10 best anchor b, the change is `actor_sign*(delta_b-delta_a)`. It describes a stable-best gap if b remains best; tied anchors are retained individually.
