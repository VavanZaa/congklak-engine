# Traced handoffs and prospective reservations

This stage implements provenance reconstruction, complete native ownership
tracing, recoverable live handoffs and jointly selected metadata reservations.
The control, candidate, relay model, policy and default engine stay fixed. There
are no optimizer steps, new teacher labels or production arena games.

## Evidence and limits

The reconstruction commits 354 source-bound units. It replays 550,846 saved moves
and records 661,153 actual origin/state incidence observations. All 21,073 legacy
components can be reconstructed from the declared witnesses, without opaque
union fallbacks. Origin vertices remain present when low-seed edges are cut.
The owned domain includes every complete state with at least 25 active seeds,
including 75–98. Observations at 24 or fewer remain in the shared ledger.

The certificate covers the declared saved sources. Historical search leaves,
feature probes and TT states that were never recorded cannot be reconstructed;
this is not a claim of historically unseen positions. The versioned role
adapter corrects historical training-cache tags while preserving actual fit,
arena and forensic exclusions. The original reconstruction stays immutable.

The 512 existing support roots span 193 reconstructed high-domain components.
Only 192 roots have no genuine optimizer exclusion; 320 share actual ancestry
with protected material. All 512 identities stay reserved as the unchanged
historical support pool, with training admission on HOLD. The pool is not
silently reduced, enlarged or represented as 512 independent components.

The existing metadata cannot supply the new fit and capture/bonus arena quotas.
A separate fixed synthetic population therefore requires an independent
pre-draw review binding its constructor, counter budget, classifier, graph,
matcher, salts and exact quotas. Every attempted root and legal child probe,
including rejected attempts, contributes to ownership before joint matching.
The intended new roles are 128 fit roots, a 32-case subset of that fit set and 24
arena origins. These custom starts make no claim of reachability from the
standard opening. Reserving them does not authorize targets or optimization.

A supplementary graph protects every newly persisted engineering trace as well.
Some earlier native smoke attempts and one failed timing harness did not retain
their full traces. Their missing footprints are explicitly recorded as a coverage
limitation. New reservations are therefore conditional metadata reservations,
disjoint from the known recorded graph; they do not certify absence of every
earlier engineering overlap or admit training/evaluation. No missing edges are
invented to close that gap.

The byte-exact V2 producer completed the fixed 16,384-counter scan in 256 committed
chunks: 73,789 legal action probes and 90,173 root/child incidences. Joint matching

## Live worker

The worker writes immutable events through a hash-linked commit journal:

1. A game-start event binds the literal state and frozen schedule unit.
2. The first played state at 24 or fewer seeds commits a handoff before another
   search. A terminal crossing records the boundary and performs no search.
3. Both learned inference controls are actually replaced before each decision;
   baseline TT/cache objects live for one decision. Native generation counters
   attest replacement. The suffix learned seat uses frozen control, while the
   opposite seat retains its assigned H_v1 or H8 relay CPU baseline.
4. A decision commits the chosen action, independent literal transition, full
   packed trace, reset evidence and a pending ownership-check cursor.
5. The ownership check commits before another search. After interruption, this
   saved check finishes before any native provider is constructed. A conflict
   retains the incident and holds the entire run.
6. A final catalog is the completion marker. Completed verification is read
   only and runs before a writer, engine or Torch import.

The contained worker has a separate Windows Job Object watchdog, cumulative
durable leases, a 4 GiB private-commit limit and a 5 GiB namespace quota. Unclean
leases retain their entire reservation. Handoff resets never reset resource
accounting. The dashboard observes immutable events and catalog files, rather
than active mutable ledgers.

The first live fixed-work fixture completed 9 consumed-state games, 144 decisions,
468,652 complete trace events and 9 handoffs, including one terminal boundary.
It covers both profiles, both baselines and both seat assignments. These are
engineering fixtures with no strength or data-freshness interpretation.

The corrected V2 launcher also completed 2 timed low-seed fixture games with 15
decisions. Actual interruption tests passed at 3 provenance publication points,
5 worker handoff/decision/catalog points and 3 data publication points. Resumed
fixed-work results match fresh runs, committed bytes and modification times stay
unchanged, and native generation counters show no repeated committed searches.
The earlier supervisor routing failure remains recorded with its full 300-second
unclean lease charge.

## Native tracing

The new DLL and Python search fork retain complete 98-seed states at actual
normalization, legal-move, terminal, transition, search, TT, feature, forward,
sidecar and PV operations. Seeds-in-hand snapshots are never canonicalized.
Packed 24-byte events retain raw stores and controller. Operation counts are
cross-checked against physical search and inference work; missing, overflowed
or resealed incomplete traces are refused.

Ownership quotients are only graph identities. Evaluator caching uses full raw
states and immutable model/rules/backend context. The H8 cache fork therefore
has slightly different physical cache work; fixed-depth scores, action Q values
and PVs remain equal in conformance fixtures.

Full trace capture and validation currently prevents production 25 ms admission
on branching positions: native author stress checks measured about 306 ms for
H_v1 and 259 ms for H8 despite nominal 25 ms requests. These are reported overruns.
They are not equal-time strength passes. The next timing work must account for
trace finalization and ownership validation before admitting a production
schedule. See [native implementation notes](NATIVE_OWNERSHIP_TRACE_IMPLEMENTATION.md).
