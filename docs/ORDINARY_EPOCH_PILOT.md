# Ordinary-only value pilot

This is a new development experiment using the accepted ordinary-only reservation epoch. It compares a new value branch with the fixed improved-policy control. The policy, original 12,872 network parameters, search arithmetic and both opponents remain frozen. The previous value candidate remains quarantined and H_v1 remains the default.

Training uses exactly the 192 admitted ordinary roots. The four actor/active-seed cells contain 39, 34, 58 and 61 roots. Each draw selects one cell with probability one quarter, then one root uniformly from that cell. All loss weights are one. There is no invented capture weight or replacement for the excluded 320 roots.

The sole target is the original completed H_v1 choice-depth-8 full-legal-action player-zero seed margin. This is a finite-horizon teacher estimate, not solved minimax and not the learned H8 relay opponent's prediction. The target is normalized as `(margin - store_difference) / signed_active_seeds`, without clipping. Accepted cached features and labels retain their historical exposure limitations. Fresh evaluation labels use the same teacher definition and full raw-state identity, including both stores and actor.

The original labels used a 512 MiB process memory limit. New traced teacher queries use a 4 GiB process resource limit because complete tracing and ownership tables themselves exceed 512 MiB; this changes the resource ceiling, not the completed depth-eight target function. The single new teacher node cap is one million and raw trace cap is sixteen million events. A required label must finish every legal action at depth eight. Fit diagnostics use full-precision native features and strict scalar-double arithmetic, matching inference; the inherited training cache remains FP32. New feature probes require their own complete ownership traces.

A zero-output 2,065-parameter branch is initialized once with seed 20261016. Training performs 400 CPU FP32 AdamW steps, batch 128, learning rate 0.0003, no weight decay, Huber delta 0.1 and gradient norm clipping at one. The dedicated sampler seed is 20261016. Only step 400 is eligible for evaluation; there is no checkpoint selection, reseeding or automatic second treatment. Checkpoints retain optimizer moments, every relevant RNG, draw order/digest and the frozen base tensors. Initial, every twentieth and any cooperative-stop checkpoints remain available.

Evaluation keeps all 128 reserved fit roots, with the 32 case roots as a subset. No evaluation root enters gradients. Capture and bonus coverage stays in evaluation. The arena uses all 24 reserved origins, both model profiles, H_v1 and H8 relay CPU, and both seats: 192 games. The critical decision budget is 25 ms with the accepted common 12 ms reserve and minimum nominal search window of 10 ms. This is not an end-to-end archival latency guarantee. Every decision resets search/cache state. At 24 or fewer active seeds the learned seat switches to the same frozen control suffix.

Complete raw native search, feature, hypothetical and TT traces cover high states through 98 active seeds. Known whole-component protections apply to every trace. Unknown high quotients are durably claimed by the reserved origin before another query. Both arms and seats of one arena origin share that origin's owner. A cross-role/component bridge, trace overflow, required incomplete teacher horizon, or unknown unfinished native query holds the campaign. Roots are never silently filtered or replaced after an outcome.

The fixed fit gates require lower overall normalized Huber loss, no higher overall seed MAE and no higher capture seed MAE than control. Capture case regret must not increase. Shared endgame value/policy/search behavior must remain exact for identical work, including the inherited exact endgame guard. Arena coverage, trace validity, legal terminal completion and absence of unexplained timing overruns are required before strength interpretation. Report paired origin comparisons against each baseline, including uncertainty; retain negative results. No automatic promotion is authorized by this pilot.

Prospective numerical tolerances are a Huber improvement greater than 1e-12, and at most 1e-9 excess seed MAE or regret. Native/reference value tolerance is 1e-9 and policy-logit tolerance is 1e-12. Shared low-state bypass and identical fixed-work decisions require exact agreement. Every critical decision exceeding 25 ms fails the equal-time interpretation gate; all overshoots and their games remain recorded. The four primary margin contrasts are candidate absolute and candidate-minus-control against each baseline. Average the two seats within each origin, give the 24 origins equal weight, and use 20,000 paired percentile bootstrap resamples with seed 20261016. Each two-sided interval has nominal 98.75% coverage (Bonferroni across four contrasts). A positive strength result requires all four lower bounds above zero and all regression, ownership and timing gates. These are exploratory intervals over custom starts, with no demonstrated population coverage calibration.

The new experiment has its own disclosed resource ledger: at most three cumulative worker hours in five-minute durable leases, 4 GiB private commit per worker and 20 GiB in the new namespaces. Previously charged budgets are retained. An independent Windows Job watchdog binds the actual worker executable, creation identity, exact arguments, source and frozen plan before native/Torch execution. Unclean exits charge the whole reservation; unverifiable overrun holds accounting. Returned saved archives are adopted before new queries; unfinished queries without a complete archive are not retried.

Independent acceptance covers the actual frozen recipe, native/export conformance, policy bytes, deterministic optimizer/RNG recovery, kill boundaries, complete evaluation ownership, both baselines and prior-artifact preservation. Known inherited training exposure and missing historical provenance remain explicit. These results cannot establish historically unseen generalization, human rank, Elo or a solved opening.

The recovery matrix is declared before execution. Two training kills bracket the step-four fixture checkpoint catalog, with an uninterrupted six-step reference and independent literal replay. Six native label fixtures kill before/after decision, ownership-check and catalog commits. Their first-fit-root footprints must be subsets already owned by that same origin in the complete labels prefix. An extra intent test kills before native entry and retains a zero-query HOLD; it is never retried. A mandatory step-zero native conformance fixture checks features, policy/value predictions and identical fixed-128 search on the first fit root before production training.

All later queries extend one fixed ownership chain: labels, initial conformance, final conformance, capture/endgame guards, two one-game handoff recovery fixtures, then the primary arena. Each handoff fixture uses the first salted arena origin with the final candidate against H_v1 in seat zero; one kills before and the other after the handoff commit. Their complete claims remain predecessors of the main arena. These two engineering games are separate from the predeclared 192 primary games, giving 194 scheduled games in total. No fixture has an ownership exemption. Actual kills share the one resource ledger and are charged their full five-minute lease.

The dashboard displays the sealed evidence status with compact current source, model and immutable receipt checks. Independent audit, the final combined seal and explicit full verification check every bound byte, including raw traces and claim blobs. Dashboard polling does not repeatedly hash all archival gigabytes and does not use modification times as an integrity substitute. Completed query/training admission keeps its strict artifact validation.

Before any new native query or optimizer update, preparation exposed repeated hashing of the same teacher database for all 192 roots. The prospective continuation deduplicates the resolved file inventory before hashing and verifies common ownership inputs once per finite batch, preserving every unique expected digest, per-origin check and the scientific recipe. Constructor-issued snapshots prevent unsupported caller-manufactured batches. The original and second plans, independent reviews, original cache, initialization and exact source bytes are retained.
