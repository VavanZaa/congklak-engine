    Choose a nonempty pit on your side. Sow around both rows, skipping your opponent's store. An occupied final pit relays its seeds; a final seed in your own store gives another turn. An empty pit on your side captures its nonempty opposite. An empty row passes, and the round ends when both rows are empty.

    Sequential opening: a verified chain of 14 bonus choices banks 51 of 98 seeds before the opponent can move. The starter can force a win in this exact single-round profile by following that chain. Other opening rules need separate analysis.

    This table follows our explicitly defined seven-pit, sequential, single-round Betawi profile. The Ministry describes one player starting; other published descriptions use simultaneous starts. Those openings are not interchangeable. Simultaneous openings, refills and closed pits are outside this version. Move history and completed search depths are saved locally. Live analysis resumes the current saved root after a restart. Stop turns live analysis off. A live search is bounded to one hour or depth 128; you can move while it is thinking.
